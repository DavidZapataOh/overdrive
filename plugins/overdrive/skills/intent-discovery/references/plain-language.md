# Plain language

The reader may know nothing about software. Every message in discovery follows these.

- **Define on first use.** A term the user did not use themselves gets its meaning in everyday words the first time it appears, in the same sentence. A term they said they do not understand is explained before any option uses it. If it can be avoided, avoid it.
- **One idea per paragraph.** Short paragraphs; no table or nested list to carry an explanation.
- **Consequence for the user.** Each option says what changes for their business or customers: what they see, what it costs, how long, what can fail. Never only how it works inside.
- **The user's words and language.** Keep the language they write in. Use their names for things (clientas, referidos), not the code's.
- **Do not stack questions to explain.** Explaining is part of the one question; it does not add a second one.
