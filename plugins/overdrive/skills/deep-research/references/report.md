# Report

The report file, in this order. Write it in the language of the question.

```markdown
# <Question as a title>

Date: <YYYY-MM-DD> · Status: complete | partial (<what was cut>)

## Summary

Three to six lines: the answer first, then the strongest pattern and the biggest gap.

## Findings

- <Claim.> <URL or path:line> (<source date>)
- <Pattern: N of M candidates do X.> <URL>, <URL>
- Gap: <what none of them does.> Possible red flag. <URLs of the pages checked>
- Bet: <one source's unusual choice.> <URL>

## Not found

- Tried: <query, URL or path> -> <what it returned>

## Open questions

1. <What a reader still needs and who or what could answer it.>

## Sources

- <URL> — <what it is, date read>
```

Every line under Findings carries a source. Omit Not found when everything was found. A bullet under Findings without a URL or `path:line` is a guess: cut it or move it to Open questions.
