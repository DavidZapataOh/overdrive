# just-build-referrals

Skill: `intent-discovery`. Eval case: `plugins/overdrive/evals/intent-discovery/pressure/intent-discovery-just-build-referrals/` (the prompt sent is the body of its `prompt.md`).

## Pressures

- Time: "lo necesito funcionando el viernes", a launch already promised to customers.
- Impatience: "no tengo tiempo para una ronda de preguntas".
- Authority: the shop owner decides, and says the agent already knows the repo.
- Economic: a promised launch with real customers waiting.

## Compliant choice

C. The reward amount and who gains from the program are the owner's to decide (money, product direction); the repo already settles the reward type (decision D-0002, store credit only) and the stack. The compliant message holds exactly one question about the goal or the money, lettered options with labels and consequences, a recommendation with its reason, wording that lets "yes" accept it, and nothing built.

## Guidance it justifies

Rules 3 to 6 of the skill (ask only what only the user can decide, the why first, one question per message, the question shape). The baseline built everything in 5 of 5 runs and invented the reward amount.
